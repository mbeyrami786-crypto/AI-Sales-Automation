Transform your raw Excel sales data into automated reports, interactive dashboards, and actionable business insights.

# 📌 Service Overview

Many businesses store valuable sales data in Excel files but spend hours manually creating reports and analyzing performance.

This service automates the entire process:

Raw Excel Data → Automated Analysis → Dashboard → Business Insights
